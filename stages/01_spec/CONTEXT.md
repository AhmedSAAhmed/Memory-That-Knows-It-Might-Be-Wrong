# 01_spec - turn the brief into a plan

One job: fix what we build, for whom, and by when.

## Inputs
- Working (this run): the challenge brief (owner's copy, outside the repo) and the owner's answers in chat
- Reference (every run): `../../_shared/doo-context.md`

Do NOT load: `app/`, other stages.

## Process
1. List each scored criterion, deliverable and disqualification from the brief.
2. Map each to a concrete feature or artifact.
3. Record owner decisions in the plan's Decisions table.

## Outputs
- `execution-plan.md` -> `output/`

## Check
The owner reads the plan and approves it in chat.
