# Doo context - who we build for

Factory reference: the domain every prompt, persona and demo scenario follows.

## The company

Doo is an AI customer-service company working with businesses in the GCC. Its agents answer customers on chat and messaging on behalf of a brand. Memory decides whether a returning customer is greeted as a stranger, as a known customer, or, worst case, with confidently wrong details.

## Why memory is risky here

- Customers move, change numbers, switch language, and close issues: support facts go stale fast.
- A wrong address or a wrong promise ("your refund was approved") costs money and trust.
- Customer data in the GCC falls under privacy laws (UAE PDPL, KSA PDPL): consent, access and erasure must be real features.

## Demo brand and persona (fictional)

- Brand: **Qamar Home**, a fictional home-goods store delivering across the UAE.
- Agent: **Doo Assist**, Qamar Home's support agent.
- Customer: **Layla**, a returning customer. Seed memories cover language, delivery city, contact preference, an open damaged-sofa ticket, and an agent promise of a refund.

All names are invented; no real Doo branding or customer data is used.

## Memory categories that matter most

`language`, `contact_pref`, `location` (delivery city), `product_owned`, `open_issue`, `commitment` (what an agent promised), `preference`, `sentiment`.
